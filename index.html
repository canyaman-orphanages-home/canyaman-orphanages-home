About Canyaman Orphanages Home

A place for people who want to stand with children and help create brighter possibilities.

Our Mission

Canyaman Orphanages Home is presented as a charitable initiative focused on supporting orphaned children. We believe children deserve dignity, care, education and opportunities to build a positive future.

Support for Children

Donations and community support can be directed toward children's needs and wellbeing.

Daily Care

Support can help with essential day-to-day needs such as food, clothing and personal care.

Education

Support can contribute toward learning materials, school-related needs and educational opportunities.

Wellbeing

Support can help provide a safer and more caring environment where children can grow.
